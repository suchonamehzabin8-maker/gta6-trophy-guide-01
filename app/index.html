    Roadmap to the platinum

    Six phases, in order, from the first mission to the last collectible. About 200 hours all told — most of it in the final phase.

      Before you start

      • Everything is earned offline in story mode. There are no online trophies and no mission replay.
      • One playthrough is enough: free roam stays open after the story ends.
      • Cheats switch trophies and saving off for that session — keep them for a throwaway save.

      Missables

      Almost nothing in GTA 6 can be missed — free roam stays open after the credits. There is exactly one exception.

        How to stay safe

        Meditate at Jason’s house the first chance you get in Chapter 1. After that, the other 17 meditation spots can be done any time. Everything else on the list waits for you in free roam.
