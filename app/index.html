      Roadmap to the platinum

      Six phases, in order, adding up to roughly 200 hours. Do the story first, grab the one missable meditation in Chapter 1, and leave the long grinds — shooting range golds, collectibles, 100% — for free roam after the credits.

      Missables

      Free roam stays open after the story, so almost nothing in this list can be missed. There is exactly one trophy the guides flag as possibly missable — and five more whose exact trigger is still unconfirmed, so this guide guesses nothing about them.
